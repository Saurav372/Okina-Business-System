## 7. Sales Model

### B2B Sales Flow

1. Customer discovers Okina Craft
2. Customer views products, examples, or advertisements
3. Customer contacts Okina Craft
4. Requirements are collected
5. Quantity, apparel type, sizes, colors, artwork, printing method, placement, and deadline are confirmed

## 33. Product and Order Costing Example

A historical costing example:

### Customer

**Deepak Mobile**

### Order

**10 T-shirts @ ₹295 each**

### Historical Cost Inputs

- T-shirt purchase cost: ₹100/piece
- DTF sheet: ₹140/meter
- Print cost: ₹2/piece
- Packing: ₹10/piece

### Revenue

10 × ₹295 = **₹2,950**

### Listed Cost

T-shirts:

10 × ₹100 = **₹1,000**

DTF:

1 × ₹140 = **₹140**

Print:

10 × ₹2 = **₹20**

Packing:

10 × ₹10 = **₹100**

**Total listed cost: ₹1,260**

### Gross Contribution

₹2,950 - ₹1,260 = **₹1,690**

Approximate gross contribution per T-shirt:

**₹169**

Approximate listed gross contribution margin:

**57.29%**

This is a historical example and excludes other possible costs such as labor, electricity, wastage, delivery, taxes, advertising, platform fees, and overhead.
