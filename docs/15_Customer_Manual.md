# Customer Manual

> **Last Reviewed:** 2026-07-02
> **Owner:** Customer Support
> **Source of Truth:** Live customer website

---

## Overview

This manual is for customers using the Okina Craft website. It covers how to browse products, customise your order, check out, track your order, and manage your account.

---

## Creating an Account

1. Click **Sign Up** on the website.
2. Enter your name, email address, phone number, and a password.
3. Click **Create Account**.
4. You will receive a confirmation — you can now log in.

### Logging In
1. Click **Log In** at the top of the page.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Log In** — you will be taken to your dashboard.

### Forgot Password
Click **Forgot Password** on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

---

## Browsing Products

- Use the navigation menu or search bar to explore product categories.
- Click a category to see all products in it.
- Products marked **Bulk Only** cannot be ordered directly — see [Bulk Orders](#bulk-orders).
- Products marked **Out of Stock** may not be available for immediate purchase; contact us to confirm availability.

---

## Product Pages

Each product page shows:

- Product description, materials, and available options (sizes, colours, print methods).
- A customization section where you can upload your design file and choose placement.
- A size-wise quantity selector for ordering different quantities per size.
- A mockup preview (approximate visual of your design applied to the product).

---

## Uploading Your Design

1. On the product page, click **Upload Design**.
2. Select your design file (PDF, PNG, or JPG — maximum 5 MB).
3. Your file is uploaded securely.
4. Choose the print placement and print method from the available options.
5. The system generates a preview mockup for your reference.

> ⚠️ The mockup is for reference only. Final production quality may vary based on print method and material. Your original file is always preserved.

---

## Adding to Cart

1. Select your product options and quantities.
2. Click **Add to Cart**.
3. You can add multiple products to the same cart.

To view your cart, click the cart icon at the top of the page.

To update quantities or remove items, use the cart page controls.

---

## Checkout

1. Open your cart and click **Proceed to Checkout**.
2. Select or add a delivery address.
3. Review your order summary and total.
4. Click **Place Order** to proceed to payment.

> ℹ️ If your total quantity across all items is **25 or more**, your order will be routed to our bulk quotation team. You will see a message and our team will contact you within 1–2 business days.

---

## Payment

1. After placing your order, you will be redirected to the secure payment page.
2. Complete payment using your preferred method (credit/debit card, UPI, net banking).
3. On successful payment, you will see an order confirmation page with your order reference number.
4. A confirmation will be sent to your email.

If the payment fails, your order is saved as **Pending Payment** — you can retry payment from your account dashboard.

---

## Order Tracking

1. Log in to your account.
2. Click **My Orders**.
3. Click any order to open its detail view.

The order detail shows:

- **Order status** — where your order is in the process (Confirmed, In Production, Shipped, etc.).
- **Payment summary** — amount paid and outstanding balance (if any).
- **Tracking information** — courier name and tracking number once dispatched.

### Order Status Meanings

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Pending Payment** | Payment has not been received yet |
| **Confirmed** | Payment received, order confirmed |
| **In Production** | Your items are being printed/manufactured |
| **Ready to Ship** | Production complete, awaiting dispatch |
| **Shipped** | Order has been dispatched with the courier |
| **Delivered** | Order has been delivered |
| **Cancelled** | Order has been cancelled |

## Design Approval

For some orders, we may contact you to confirm the final design before production begins. When design approval is required:

1. You will receive a notification (email or WhatsApp).
2. Log in, open the order, and review the design notes.
3. Contact us to confirm approval.

Once approved, production begins and you will be notified.

## Managing Your Account

### Updating Profile Information
1. Log in and click **My Account** or your name at the top of the page.
2. Update your name, phone number, or email address.
3. Save your changes.

### Managing Addresses
1. Go to **My Account → Addresses**.
2. Click **Add Address** to save a new delivery address.
3. You can have multiple saved addresses and select one at checkout.

---

## Bulk Orders

If you need 25 or more items (across all sizes and products in a single order):

1. Browse and add products to your cart as normal.
2. At checkout, if the total quantity is 25 or more, the checkout will route your enquiry to our bulk team.
3. You will see a message: *"Our team will contact you to prepare a quotation."*
4. A Sales Staff member will contact you within 1–2 business days.

### Quotation Approval

When a quotation is ready:

1. You will receive an email with a quotation link.
2. Review the items, quantities, and agreed prices.
3. Click **Approve Quotation** to confirm.
4. Once approved, your Sales Order is created and our team will request an advance payment.

Please have your **order reference number** ready when contacting support.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I change my order after placing it?**
Contact us as soon as possible. Changes may not be possible once production has started.

**My payment failed — what do I do?**
Log in, go to **My Orders**, open the order, and use the payment option to retry.

**Can I cancel my order?**
Cancellations depend on the production stage. Contact us immediately if you need to cancel.

**How long does delivery take?**
Delivery times depend on the product and shipping location. Your order detail will show the estimated delivery date once dispatched.

**Is my uploaded design file secure?**
Yes. Uploaded files are stored in private, encrypted storage and are only accessible to you and authorised Okina Craft staff.
